                Navigating MACT (Motor Accident Claims) & Insurance Defense
              

              
            
            

              Proceedings before the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT), such as those at the 10th ADJ Alipore or Uluberia Court, are highly specialized. For claimants, establishing negligence and calculating just compensation relies heavily on strict adherence to the Sarla Verma and Pranay Sethi multiplier guidelines. For insurance defense (such as representations for Oriental Insurance Co. Ltd.), mitigating institutional liability requires forensic scrutiny of statutory breaches—including unverified driving licenses, expired route permits, or delayed FIR registrations. We ensure our clients are thoroughly prepared for the evidentiary demands of the Tribunal.
            

          


          

            
              

                Civil & Property Title Suits (Focus on District Courts)
              

              
            
            

              Property disputes in Kolkata and South 24 Parganas require exhaustive due diligence. Filing or defending a Title Suit, Partition Suit, or seeking an Order 39 Injunction in the District Courts demands an unbroken chain of title. Our forensic approach involves deep 30-year searches through Mother Deeds at the ADSR/DSR, cross-referencing LR & RS Khatian records via Banglarbhumi, and authenticating municipal mutations. Whether it is an eviction proceeding or a complex declaration of ownership, our pleadings are drafted to withstand rigorous cross-examination and appellate scrutiny.
            

          


          

            
              

                Statutory Compliance: Fire Safety & Police Submissions for PGs/Residential Accommodations
              

              
            
            

              Operating a Residential Paying Guest (PG) accommodation or leasing property in Kolkata involves strict adherence to local laws to avoid penal liabilities under the BNS (formerly IPC). Property owners must complete mandatory Police Tenant / Occupant Verification filings (often guided by the Sarai Act or local Police Commissionerate orders), secure Municipal Trade Licenses (Certificate of Enlistment), and maintain valid Fire Safety NOCs and operational extinguishers. Lex Consuasor audits premises compliance and drafts robust Leave & License agreements to legally protect landlords from unauthorized commercial use or tenancy disputes.
