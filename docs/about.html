About the practice

A steady, thoughtful approach to legal work.

Lex Consuasor is a Kolkata-based legal practice established with the aim of providing dependable counsel across a range of civil, commercial and regulatory matters.

Our perspective

Professional guidance, grounded in context.

Every matter has its own facts, risks and practical considerations. We take time to understand those details before advising on the available course of action. This allows our work to remain focused, considered and appropriate to the brief.

We believe clients benefit from timely communication and a clear explanation of legal process. Our team works to provide that clarity at each stage of an engagement.
