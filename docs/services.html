Our services

Support across the legal issues that shape your next move.

Our services are tailored to the facts and requirements of the individual matter. The overview below is intended as a general guide to our areas of work.

01

Business & corporate law

We advise on business arrangements, corporate documentation, commercial contracts and governance-related questions. Our approach is practical, with attention to the commercial context as well as the legal framework.

02

Property & real estate

We provide support in relation to property transactions, documentation, due diligence and issues arising from ownership or occupation.

03

Tax & regulatory matters

We assist clients in understanding tax and regulatory requirements relevant to their circumstances, and in preparing for the appropriate process.

04

Employment & labour law

We offer guidance on workplace documentation, employment relationships and related questions for employers and individuals.

05

Intellectual property

We help clients consider the protection, use and management of intellectual property in connection with their business or work.

06

Dispute support

Where a dispute arises, we assist with an informed assessment of the matter and the available procedural options.

A considered start

Discussing a new matter.

Before accepting an engagement, we consider the nature of the matter and conduct appropriate conflict checks. An initial discussion can help establish whether our services are suitable for your requirements.
